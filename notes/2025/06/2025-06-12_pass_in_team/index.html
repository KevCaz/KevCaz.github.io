Using pass in a team

 June 12, 2025

  password pass cli

I use pass for my password management everyday. I use it for my own usage, but the need to share passowrd with my team has become more important. I decided to see whether it was possible to use pass to do it and stumbled into the post ‘Using pass in a team’ by David on Medium.

This provided all the steps to configure a shared repository. The goal of this post is not to rephrase what David explains throughout his posts but to complement and clarify what was not clear in the original post.
